## ✨ Overview

The **Patient Health Risk Predictor and Tracker** is an interactive, web-based health monitoring platform built as part of the **Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI)** graduation project[cite: 1, 4]. 

Instead of leaving users with raw numbers and confusing logs, our system collects daily vitals and lifestyle habits, visualizes historical trends, and runs predictive machine learning models to highlight potential health risks early on[cite: 4, 5].

---

## 🎯 Key Features

- 👤 **User Health Profile:** Store personal metrics such as age, biological sex, height, weight, and existing conditions[cite: 7].
- 🩺 **Daily Vital Tracking:** Log daily metrics including heart rate, blood pressure, blood glucose, sleep duration, and physical activity[cite: 7].
- 📊 **Interactive Dashboard:** Beautiful visual cards, charts, and summary tables powered by React[cite: 7, 8].
- 📈 **Historical Trend Analysis:** Track long-term variations in weight, blood pressure, and risk scores[cite: 8].
- 🚨 **AI Risk Prediction & Alerts:** Automated classification into risk levels (e.g., *Low Risk → Moderate Risk → Higher Risk*) with clear, non-technical explanations[cite: 8, 11].
